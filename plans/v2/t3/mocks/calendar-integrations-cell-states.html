The test to pass

Three questions, before we build.

If any of these answers is "not quite", the inline model isn't ready to replace the preview page yet.

1 · Can you tell suggested-free from confirmed-free at a glance?

At phone distance, in bright sunlight, on a cracked screen. If someone taps a suggestion thinking it's already committed, the whole premise collapses.

2 · Does "suggested busy" feel tappable?

The strikethrough risks reading as disabled. The hint is "we think you're busy, but you can override" — if users don't realise they can tap, they'll miss every override case.

3 · Do suggestions survive next to participant dots?

A busy shared calendar has 4–5 coloured dots per cell. The suggestion language (dashed / hatched) has to stay legible under that noise. The two-week mock above is the test case.
