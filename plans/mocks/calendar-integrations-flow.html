Connecting Google Calendar

Just so you know what we'll do.

This is a one-shot check, not an ongoing sync. Here's exactly what happens:

◌ What we see Whether each day has space — nothing about titles, attendees, or locations.
◌ What we keep Nothing. The check happens once and we don't store the result.
◌ What changes for the group Your free days get suggested — you confirm each one before anything is shared.
Tapping a suggestion makes it part of your availability. Revoking Google access later won't undo days you've already confirmed.

FreeWhen-voice screen, not Google's. Doubles as our Article 13 informed-consent record — Continue writes users.calendar_import_consent_ack_at and logs calendar_consent_given (no PII) before redirecting. Once set, this screen never shows again.
